## 1. Scope of Application

This policy applies to the VoisWork App and its associated services. It covers:

1.1 Account information provided by your organization's administrator when your work account is created, and information you provide when using the App;  
1.2 Information automatically collected when you use the App, including device information, IP address, and log data;  
1.3 The content you send or receive through the App, including messages, voice, photos, videos, files, and location.

Third-party services integrated in the App process data under their own privacy policies; they are listed with links in Section 2.

This App is a communication and management tool for enterprise users (push-to-talk intercom, messaging, location sharing, and task dispatch within an organization). Employee accounts are provisioned and managed by your organization's administrator; this Policy applies to our processing of your personal data through the App. This App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 2. How We Collect and Use Personal Information

We collect personal information to provide services and ensure compliance with applicable laws. While providing information is voluntary, without it we may be unable to deliver certain services or address your inquiries.

1  Account and authentication: Your work account is created by your organization's administrator; the App has no self-registration. We use your user ID and display name and, where bound to your account, your phone number or email address, to authenticate logins and deliver verification codes (SMS or email) for identity verification, password reset, and account management. If you do not provide the verification code, the corresponding operation cannot complete.

2  Device and log information: We collect your device identifier (Android ID), device model, operating system version, network status, IP address, and access/event logs to bind your account to your device, keep your login session secure, and diagnose faults. We do not collect your IMEI, SIM IMSI, OAID, or advertising identifier, and we do not read your contacts, call logs, SMS messages, or installed-app list.

3  Content you send and receive: Text messages, voice messages, photos, videos, files, location messages, and group information you send are relayed through our servers to deliver them to the recipients you select and to sync across your devices, and are also stored locally on your device. You can enable "auto clear chat history" in Settings to periodically delete local history.

4  Location: For GPS-based services such as mapping, check-in, real-time location sharing, and route/track recording, subject to your organization's configuration. When you explicitly grant "Allow all the time", we also collect location while the App is in the background, solely to support the location sharing or track recording session initiated by you or your organization; a separate in-app disclosure is shown before that permission is requested. Background location is not used for advertising or profiling, and you can disable it at any time in system settings.

5  Service Permissions: Enabling these permissions authorizes us to collect corresponding data. Disabling them will affect related services:

a) Microphone: For push-to-talk intercom and voice messages, including talking through a connected Bluetooth hand microphone. Your voice is recorded only while you hold the intercom button or record a voice message, and is transmitted only to the groups or contacts you select.

b) Camera: For QR code scanning and for taking photos or videos to share.

c) Photos and files: When you send photos or videos, you select them in the system photo picker and the App accesses only the items you pick there (it fully supports Android's "select photos" limited access). Files you choose to send as work files are accessed only through the system file picker. Voice messages and other media are saved in the App's own private storage on your device. On Android versions older than 10, saving exported files to the shared Download folder additionally requires the storage permission.

d) Nearby devices (Bluetooth): Bluetooth is used only to discover and connect your VoisWork Bluetooth accessories (e.g., PTT buttons and Bluetooth hand microphones); it is not used to infer your physical location. On Android 12 and above, discovering and connecting to Bluetooth devices requires the "Nearby devices" permission; the location permission is requested only because some devices require it for Bluetooth scanning.

e) Notifications: With your permission (requested on Android 13 and above), we send notifications for new messages and service alerts.

6  What we do not do: We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for third-party advertising. This App contains no advertising SDKs, does not collect or use advertising identifiers, and does not start automatically when the device powers on.

## 3. How We Store Personal Information

The information collected about you by this App will be stored on the servers of this App and/or its affiliated companies. Such information may be transmitted to, accessed from, stored in, and displayed in countries or regions outside your country of residence or where the information was collected.

Data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Location track data is retained only for the period needed to provide track features per your organization's configuration. Chat databases, credentials, and business files are excluded from device cloud backups (including Google Drive backup) and device-to-device migration, so your messages and login credentials are not copied to cloud backups.

## 4. How We Share, Transfer, and Disclose Personal Information

We disclose your personal information only in the following circumstances:

1  With your prior consent (including but not limited to electronic confirmation of consent);

2  Within your organization: information you send (messages, voice, location, files) is delivered to the colleagues, groups, or channels you select;

3  To service providers: we use service providers to operate the service, including cloud infrastructure, message relaying, file storage, SMS/email delivery of verification codes, and the map/location services listed in Section 2. These providers process data on our behalf;

4  To supervisory, administrative, or judicial authorities in accordance with legal requirements or upon their request.

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

## 5. How We Protect Personal Information Security

1  All accounts of this App have security protection features. Please keep your username and password information secure. This App employs security measures such as password encryption to prevent loss, misuse, or alteration of your information. However, please note that no "perfect security measures" exist in information networks. You should also take effective self-protection measures, such as changing your login password regularly.

2  Please also take care when sharing personal information in messages, voice, or files with other users, and disclose it only when necessary. If you discover any unauthorized disclosure of your personal information—especially your username or password for this App—please contact our customer service immediately. You may also report the incident to the police so that we can take appropriate measures.

3  Passwords are stored only in encrypted (hashed) form, and access to personal data is restricted to personnel who need it to operate the service. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 6. How to Delete Your Account

As a communication and management tool for enterprise users, the creation and deletion of employee accounts are managed by your organization's administrator. If you wish to delete your account, please contact your administrator, who can log in to the VoisWork Enterprise Management System via computer to complete the account deletion process.

VoisWork Enterprise Management System URL: https://www.voiswork.com

Operation path: Contacts → Member Management → Actions → Delete Member

The App also provides a "Delete Account" page (in-app path: Settings → Delete Account) that shows the management system address and the operation path above for your market.

Upon receiving the administrator's deletion request, we will process the account deletion based on their authorization. Please note that account deletion is irreversible. We will cease providing services to you, stop collecting your personal information, and delete or anonymize your account-related data upon request, unless otherwise required by laws, regulations, or regulatory authorities. Content already delivered to other members' devices remains visible to those users until they delete it.
