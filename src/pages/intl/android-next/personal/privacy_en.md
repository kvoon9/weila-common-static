# VoisChat Privacy Policy

- Updated: October 14, 2026
- Effective: April 8, 2025

VoisChat ("this App") is developed by Shenzhen Voit Industrial Co., Ltd. ("Weila" or "we") to provide you with voice-based real-time communication services. **We take your privacy seriously. This App will use and disclose your personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy, treating such information with the utmost diligence and prudence. Unless otherwise specified herein, we will not disclose or provide your information to third parties without your prior consent. This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. By agreeing to our Terms of Service, you are deemed to have accepted all provisions of this Privacy Policy, which forms an integral part of our Terms of Service.** If you have any questions, comments, or suggestions regarding this policy, please contact us using the information provided at the end of this document.

This policy will help you understand:

1\. Scope of Application

2\. How We Collect and Use Personal Information

3\. How We Store Personal Information

4\. How We Share, Transfer, and Disclose Personal Information

5\. How We Protect Personal Information Security

6\. How to Delete Your Account

7\. How This Privacy Policy Is Updated

8\. How to Contact Us

## 1. Scope of Application

1. Personal registration information you provide when creating an account with this App;

2. Information automatically collected when you use our services, including but not limited to your IP address, device model, operating system version, language preferences, access dates/times, and log information;

You understand and agree that anonymized or de-identified statistical information that cannot be used to identify you is not covered by this Privacy Policy.

## 2. How We Collect and Use Personal Information

We collect personal information to provide services and ensure compliance with applicable laws. While providing information is voluntary, without it we may be unable to deliver certain services or address your inquiries.

1. Device Optimization: We may collect device information (such as Android ID, device model, operating system version, and language settings) and software versions to optimize display and functionality.

2. Account Creation: We collect your email address and the email verification code you submit for identity authentication and account creation. Without these, registration will fail and you will be unable to continue using this App.

3. Communication Content: To provide the real-time communication service, we transmit and temporarily store the messages you send (text, voice, images, videos, files, and location messages), your friend and group relationships, and your paired device information (such as device ID and firmware version). Message content is stored on our servers for offline delivery and download, and is deleted after you delete your account or once the seven-day retention period expires.

4. Service Permissions: To provide complete communication services, we may access the following device permissions. Enabling these permissions authorizes us to collect corresponding data for service delivery. Disabling them will affect related services, though you can refuse or disable these permissions at any time through your phone settings without affecting other features:

**a) Microphone** You can allow this App to use the microphone when using our products/services for voice communication or voice command recognition.

**b) Storage** By enabling local storage permission, audio, images, and video content can be cached to your phone storage for convenient playback, and you can share local images and videos with other users in this App.

**c) Camera** You can use the camera to scan QR codes or share photos and videos with users in this App.

**d) Bluetooth** You can use Bluetooth functionality with our Bluetooth devices for better communication services.

**e) Location** You can allow this App to access your precise location (such as via satellite/GNSS) to share your real-time location with friends and group members, set geofences and view location history for your paired devices, and select or search locations on the map. If you refuse or disable location permission, these features will be unavailable, and other features of this App will not be affected. You can also turn off the location sharing switch in the App separately to stop sharing your location with other users only.

To better serve you, we use third-party SDKs that require certain permissions:

**a) Firebase**

Provider: Google

Purpose: Crash log statistics

Collection method: Background collection

Personal information: **Device model, operating system version, Firebase installation ID (an app instance identifier randomly generated for each app installation), IP address (for approximate location estimation), crash logs, stack traces, and the device state at the time of the crash (battery, memory, storage, screen orientation, network connection state)**

Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

**b) Alibaba Cloud Object Storage Service (OSS)**

Provider: Alibaba Cloud

Purpose: File and message storage

Collection method: Background collection

Personal information: **Device information (Android ID, device model, operating system version), network identity information: IP address (approximate location), logs**

Privacy Policy: http://terms.aliyun.com/legal-agreement/terms/suit_bu1_ali_cloud/suit_bu1_ali_cloud201902141711_54837.html?spm=a2c4g.11186623.0.0.3fc85ff2WPlDw6

**c) Google Play Services (Location & Maps)**

Provider: Google

Purpose: Obtain device location and display maps for location sharing, geofences, track playback, and map-based location picking

Collection method: Collected when you use the related features

Personal information: **approximate and precise location, IP address, device information**

Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. How We Store Personal Information

The information and data collected about you by this App will be stored on the servers of this App and/or its affiliated companies. Such information and data may be transmitted to, accessed from, stored in, and displayed in countries or regions outside your country of residence or where the information was collected.

## 4. How We Share, Transfer, and Disclose Personal Information

We will not share your information with third parties except for the service providers listed in Section 2, and under the following circumstances:

1. Disclosure to supervisory, administrative, or judicial authorities in accordance with legal requirements or upon their request;

2. Other disclosures deemed appropriate by this App in accordance with laws, regulations, or website policies.

## 5. How We Protect Personal Information Security

1. All accounts of this App have security protection features. Please keep your username and password information secure. This App employs security measures such as password encryption to prevent loss, misuse, or alteration of your information. However, please note that no "perfect security measures" exist in information networks. You should also take effective self-protection measures, such as changing your login password regularly.

2. If a personal information security incident occurs (for example, if your personal information is disclosed without authorization, tampered with, or lost), we will promptly inform you—through push notifications, email, or in-app announcements—of the affected information, the possible consequences, and the remedial measures taken, and we will report the incident to the regulatory authorities in accordance with the law. If you discover that your personal information has been leaked—especially your account password for this App—please change your password immediately and contact us using the details listed in Section 8; you may also report the incident to the police.

## 6. How to Delete Your Account

Users can choose to delete their account. The specific deletion method is to click Profile -> Account -> Deactivate Account in the App; you can also visit the website

https://voiswork.com/admin/#/user/unregist-by-email

Enter your account and password to delete your account, which takes effect immediately upon successful deletion. Please note that account deletion is irreversible. We will cease providing services to you, stop collecting your personal information, and delete or anonymize your account-related data upon request, unless otherwise required by laws, regulations, or regulatory authorities.

## 7. How This Privacy Policy Is Updated

1. If we decide to modify our privacy policy, we will publish these changes within this policy, on our company website, and in other locations we deem appropriate. This will help you understand how we collect and use your personal information, who has access to it, and under what circumstances we may disclose it.

2. We reserve the right to amend this policy at any time. Therefore, please review it regularly. For significant changes to this policy, we will update the revised version and the "Last Updated" date here and notify you through website announcements.
